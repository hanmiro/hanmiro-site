OEM Process

From idea to product
in six steps

Request a sample
  1. 01 Scope
    Agreement
    Before the contract, we sign an agreement setting project scope and terms.
  2. 02 Requirements
    Recipe
    You share your sauce recipe and development direction.
  3. 03 Review
    Plan & quote
    We plan production from your recipe and prepare a quote.
  4. 04 Tasting
    Sample
    We make samples to your recipe, then check and adjust taste and quality.
  5. 05 Confirm
    Contract
    Based on the samples, we confirm final specs and supply terms.
  6. 06 Delivery
    Production & delivery
    We produce, inspect and ship as agreed.
